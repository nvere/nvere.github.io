Penguin Hockey

Game

Game Overview

Overview

Pengin Hockey is a 2D game where the player uses w,a,s,d and the space bar to skate around an ice rink and either push or shoot a hockey puck into a goal. There is a goalie blocking the path.

Credits

Nicolette Vere - Gameplay programmer and asset creator.

Music free to use

Technical Information

Tech Stack

Godot, gdscript, and Adobe Fresco for asset creation.
