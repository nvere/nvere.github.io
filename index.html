About Me

Hello! I am a technical artist with a bachelors in Robotics Engineering and professional background in software and data engineering. I am currently enrolled in a Game Development Masters focusing on programming and art at the University of Illinois. I have a bachelor's degree in robotics engineering with 5+ years of professional experience in software engineering, data engineering, and genomics data research.

When not at work, I am creating 2D physical and digital art and creating 3D art and assets. I am passionate about making things look good and run optimally and creating complex assets and effects using advanced programming and technology. In any left over time, I play bass guitar in a jazz/psych rock fusion band, hike, and rock climb.
